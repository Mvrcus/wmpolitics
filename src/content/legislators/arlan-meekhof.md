Arlan Meekhof (R-West Olive) held the Ottawa County state Senate seat from 2011 through 2018, serving as Senate Majority Leader from 2015 until term limits ended his tenure. The seat he held was numbered District 30 at the time; the same Holland-area geography became District 31 under the maps in force since 2023.
