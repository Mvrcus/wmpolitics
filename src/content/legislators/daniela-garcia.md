Daniela Garcia (R-Holland) held Holland's state House seat, then District 90, from 2015 through 2018, leaving to run for the state Senate seat that Roger Victory won in the 2018 Republican primary. She later returned to local politics, challenging Democrat Doug Zylstra for the Ottawa County board's District 3 seat in 2022, unsuccessfully. Holland's House geography is today's District 86.
