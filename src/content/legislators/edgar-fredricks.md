Edgar Fredricks (R-Holland) held the Holland area's state Senate seat, then District 23, from 1979 through 1990. He gave up the seat to challenge U.S. Rep. Fred Upton in the 1990 Republican congressional primary, unsuccessfully. The geography he represented is today's Senate District 31.
