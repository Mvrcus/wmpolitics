Joe Haveman (R-Holland) held Holland's state House seat, then District 90, for three terms from 2009 through 2014, when term limits ended his House service. He later ran in the 2018 Republican primary for the Ottawa County state Senate seat, which Roger Victory won. Holland's House geography is today's District 86.
