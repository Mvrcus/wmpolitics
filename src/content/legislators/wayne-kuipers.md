Wayne Kuipers (R-Holland) represented Holland in the state House (District 90, 1999–2002) and then in the state Senate (District 30, 2003–2010) until term limits. He ran for Michigan's 2nd Congressional District in 2010, losing the Republican primary to Bill Huizenga. His old Senate geography is today's District 31.
