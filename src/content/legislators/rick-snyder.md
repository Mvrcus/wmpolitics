summary: >-
  Republican governor from 2011 through 2019, when term limits ended his tenure.
  The Ann Arbor-area venture capitalist campaigned as "one tough nerd," presided
  over Detroit's bankruptcy and Medicaid expansion, and left office with his
  legacy defined in large part by the Flint water crisis. Gretchen Whitmer
  succeeded him.
goodPoints:
  - title: Detroit bankruptcy and the grand bargain
    body: >-
      Took Detroit into the largest municipal bankruptcy in U.S. history in 2013
      and backed the "grand bargain"—roughly $800 million from foundations,
      corporations, and the state—that softened pension cuts and kept the Detroit
      Institute of Arts collection intact, letting the city exit bankruptcy in
      under 17 months.
    source: https://www.cbsnews.com/detroit/news/full-senate-vote-expected-on-grand-bargain-legislation/
  - title: Healthy Michigan Medicaid expansion
    body: >-
      Persuaded a Republican Legislature to expand Medicaid under the Affordable
      Care Act in 2013. The Healthy Michigan plan grew to cover roughly 660,000
      lower-income residents, far more than originally projected.
    source: https://www.detroitnews.com/story/news/local/michigan/2018/09/18/schuette-whitmer-healthy-michigan-medicaid/1292309002/
concerns:
  - title: Flint water crisis
    body: >-
      While Flint was run by his state-appointed emergency managers, the city
      switched to improperly treated Flint River water in 2014, exposing
      residents to lead and coinciding with a deadly Legionnaires' disease
      outbreak. State officials dismissed residents' complaints for months, and
      Snyder publicly apologized in 2016.
    source: https://bridgemi.com/michigan-environment-watch/flint-water-crisis-charges-dropped-against-former-michigan-gov-rick/
  - title: Criminal charges dismissed on procedural grounds
    body: >-
      Snyder was indicted in January 2021 on two misdemeanor counts of willful
      neglect of duty over Flint. The charges were dismissed in December 2022
      after the Michigan Supreme Court ruled the one-judge grand jury used to
      indict him was invalid—a procedural ruling, not a verdict on the merits.
    source: https://www.cnn.com/2022/12/13/us/flint-michigan-rick-snyder-water-crisis/
