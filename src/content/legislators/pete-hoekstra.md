Pete Hoekstra (R-Holland) held Holland's congressional seat for 18 years before Bill Huizenga. After leaving Congress he lost the 2010 gubernatorial primary and a 2012 U.S. Senate race, then served as Ambassador to the Netherlands (2018–2021) and later as Ambassador to Canada.
