# Past officeholders roster (research reference)

Verified succession lists for every seat covered by the site, compiled July 2026 to
drive the `/legislators/past/` section. Each table is grouped by the `seatSlug` used in
`src/content/legislators/` frontmatter. Names marked **current** already have profiles;
everyone else is a candidate for a `status: past` profile.

Sources: Wikipedia district/member pages, Senate.gov, Ballotpedia, the Michigan
official legislator database (mdoe.state.mi.us), the Ford Library finding aid, the
UCLA/Lewis historical congressional-boundary dataset (point-in-polygon checks for
Holland's coordinates per districting era), Ottawa County board records, and local news
(Holland Sentinel, WOODTV, WZZM, WHTC, Fox17, Michigan Advance).

## ⚠ Corrections the research surfaced

1. **Holland is not in Senate District 30.** Since the 2023 MICRC maps, the city of
   Holland is in **SD-31 (Roger Victory, R)**. Mark Huizenga's SD-30 is a Grand
   Rapids/Walker-area seat and does not include Holland. The `mark-huizenga.md`
   profile ("Michigan Senate District 30 (Ottawa & Kent)", `servesHolland`) should be
   reviewed — the site likely wants a Roger Victory profile for SD-31 instead.
2. **Thomas Albert's old HD-86 was a Kent/Ionia seat.** The number 86 only became
   Holland's House district in 2023. Albert is currently filed under
   `mi-state-house-86` — that is number-lineage, not Holland-geography. The
   geographic predecessors of Nancy DeBoer's seat are the old **District 90** holders
   (and District 55 in 1991–92). Decide one convention; this doc lists the
   geographic lineage as primary.
3. **No U.S. Senate profiles exist yet** (current or past). Slotkin (Class 1) and
   Peters (Class 2, retiring after 2026) would need current profiles before their
   predecessors can be grouped under a seat.

Allegan-County sliver of Holland (south side) sat in separate districts until 2023:
Patricia Birkholz (R, SD-24, 2003–2010), Tonya Schuitmaker (R, SD-26, 2011–2018,
term-limited), Aric Nesbitt (R, SD-26, 2019–2022, moved to new SD-20). Since 2023 all
of Holland city is unified in SD-31.

Number-lineage note for `mi-state-senate-30` (Mark Huizenga's actual seat): Victory
2019–22, Meekhof 2011–18, Kuipers 2003–10; before 2003 the number 30 was a Grand
Rapids seat (Glenn Steil Sr., R, 1995–2002) and earlier a mid-Michigan seat
(Frederick Dillingham, R, 1987–94). Mark Huizenga entered the Senate Nov 2021 via
special election in the old 28th, holding SD-30 only since 2023.
